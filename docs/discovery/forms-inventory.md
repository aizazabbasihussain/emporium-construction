# Forms inventory

One row per paper form the company uses today. Status is `awaiting` until the owner supplies a sample (input H-14); the practice columns stay `TBC` until then and are never guessed. When a sample arrives, fill the row, set the status to `received`, and complete the field mapping in the linked file. Samples must not be copied into the repository with real names, CNIC numbers, phone numbers or amounts (use synthetic values).
