# Workflow: attendance to monthly wages

About 50 workers in teams under supervisors, paid monthly. Statutory deductions (EOBI, social security) are deferred.

| Step | Actor | Document produced | Approval | Spec |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Keep worker records, pay basis, rates and teams | Office staff | Worker record | none | P1-16 |
| 2. Record daily attendance per worker per project | Supervisor | Attendance sheet | none | P1-17 |
| 3. Approve attendance | Project manager | Approved attendance | Approval per H-09 | P1-17 |
| 4. Allocate labour cost to projects by attendance days | System | Approved labour cost entries | none | P1-17 |
| 5. Calculate monthly pay (overtime, allowances, deductions, advance recovery) | Accountant | Draft wage sheet | none | P1-18 |
| 6. Review and approve the wage sheet | Owner | Approved wage sheet | Approval per H-09 | P1-18 |
| 7. Pay workers and issue payslips | Accountant | Payments and payslips | Approval; the creator cannot approve | P2-07 |
| 8. Finalize labour cost and reconcile with attendance cost | Accountant | Posted labour cost | none | P2-07 |
